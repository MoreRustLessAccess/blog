How we integrate with other apps

Gate supports OpenID connect w/OAuth2 to allow other apps to ask for access to data from your Gate account.

OIDC & OAuth2

We support both logging into other apps with your Gate account, but also logging into Gate with your Google/Microsoft/OpenStreetMap account. Adding a provider for logging into Gate is as simple as adding another entry in the backend configuration.
